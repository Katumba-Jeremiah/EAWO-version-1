We enable, connect and strengthen. We do not replace.

EAWO works with institutions that already have expertise, infrastructure or market access, and brings them together around practical agribusiness opportunities.

Ways to work with us.

Each partner brings something different. Tell us what you can offer and what you want to achieve, and we will shape the collaboration around it.

Propose a partnership

Market opportunities, technical assistance, internships, sponsorship, procurement linkages and investment.

Financial literacy, credit readiness, enterprise pipeline development and financing referrals.

Government

Policy engagement, technical collaboration, standards, market development and programme implementation.

Development partners

Programme funding, technical assistance, evidence generation, innovation and scale-up.

Media and technology

How we keep going without losing our mission.

We begin with available resources, leverage partnerships, demonstrate value, document results, then mobilise larger resources. Income is applied to our objectives, not distributed to members.

Resource mobilisation

  • Grants and donations
  • Corporate partnerships and sponsorship
  • Institutional partnerships
  • Technical assistance
  • Strategic programme funding

Earned services

  • Business planning and advisory
  • Market research
  • Corporate and enterprise training
  • Investment-readiness support
  • Export advisory and selected consultancy
