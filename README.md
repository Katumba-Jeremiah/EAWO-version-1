# EAWO Ltd website

## Brand assets
Your files were processed into `assets/brand/`: `wordmark-light.png` (header and footer, transparent background), `logo-full.png` (About page), `icon.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` and `assets/favicon.ico`. Colours are taken from the logo: deep green #04441F, leaf green #8CC63F, gold #F4B329.

## Photos
Every photo on the site is different (20 slots, no repeats across pages). Market photos are from Pexels (free licence, African market scenes, not Uganda-specific). The stock photos were chosen for Black African people and Uganda settings, but check each one on screen and swap any that don't fit.

Photos are loaded straight from the free Unsplash and Pexels image libraries, sized for each slot (small on phones, larger on big screens), with the homepage hero photo preloaded so it appears fast. To use your own photo instead, save it in `assets/photos/` with the filename from the list below and send it to whoever maintains the site to rebuild the pages (or change that image's `src` in the HTML). Own photos are also the fastest option: compress each to about 100 KB.

## Photo shot list

Add photos to `assets/photos/` using EXACTLY these filenames (.jpg, landscape, ideally 1800px wide, under 400 KB each).
Until a photo exists, the slot shows a green/gold tonal panel, so the site never looks broken.
Add `#slots` to any page address (e.g. index.html#slots) to see which file belongs where.

| File | What to photograph |
|---|---|
| hero-home.jpg | Wide shot of farmers or entrepreneurs at work (field or market). Left side is covered by text, so keep the subject right of centre |
| mentoring.jpg | A mentor and an entrepreneur reviewing records or products (portrait-friendly) |
| capacity.jpg | A training session or enterprise clinic in progress |
| markets.jpg | Market stalls, packaged products, or a buyer meeting |
| finance.jpg | Business plan or cash-flow review, pitch practice |
| demo-farm.jpg | Demonstration plot, greenhouse or irrigation |
| students.jpg | Students on placement at a host enterprise |
| agritv.jpg | Filming a farmer interview or demonstration on a phone |
| partners-band.jpg | Partners working together (used as the dark banner behind "Start a conversation") |
| partners.jpg | Stakeholders around a table |
| about-hero.jpg, about-team.jpg | EAWO team, community gathering |
| programmes-hero.jpg | Hands-on training or demonstration |
| partnerships-hero.jpg | Partners meeting |

Best source: your own photos from EAWO events. Otherwise use licence-free libraries (Unsplash, Pexels) and check each licence.
Always get consent before publishing recognisable photos of people. Use descriptive alt text (edit in the HTML).
